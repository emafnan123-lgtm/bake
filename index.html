Our Philosophy

A Love Letter to Butter, Beans & Botany

La Violette was born from a simple yet passionate vision: to recreate the slow-paced elegance of French pâtisseries blended harmoniously with the lively spirit of specialty third-wave coffee bars.

We believe that a croissant must shatter delicately upon the first bite, an espresso must possess sweet crema without bitterness, and dessert should always be savored in an inspiring, peaceful open-air garden.
